# SawEars User Guide
## What is SawEars?
SawEars is a pedagogical application aimed at players of the musical saw, or other continuous pitch instruments such as the theremin or voice.

## Getting started
The basic flow of the application is as follows:
1. From the main screen, click Start Lesson to open the song selection screen
2. Search the available options and select a song to play
3. On the Lesson Settings screen adjust lesson settings (for more detail see the Lesson Settings section of this guide) and hit "Done" to confirm
4. On the Lesson screen, hit the play button to start the lesson.
5. Click the speaker icon to hear the melody before starting the lesson (optional)
6. Click the play button to start the Lesson
7. Attempt to play each note at the correct pitch as it passes the centre of the screen. You can see your current pitch relative to each note by the height of the dot at the centre of the screen.


## Song selection screen
Enter a search term in the search bar to search available songs.

## Settings menu
The settings menu can be reached by clicking the gear icon in the top app bar. Settings are grouped in the following categories.

## Instrument
Here you can set the playable range of your instrument. This is the highest and lowest note your instrument can produce.
It's very important to set these correctly as SawEars uses this to adjust the pitch of songs to fit your saw's range!

- Range Min: The lowest note your instrument can produce (MIDI value)
- Range Max: The highest note your instrument can product (MIDI value)

## Lesson
Here you can choose settings related to the lesson itself

- Difficulty: Controls how strict the pitch tracking will be. The harder the difficulty, the more precisely you need to hit each note to be marked as accurate.
- Mode: The app has two main modes, PRACTICE and PERFORMANCE. In practice mode, the app will "wait" until you have performed each note for the expected duration before moving to the next note. In PERFORMANCE note, the app will move continuously through the song at the configured tempo.

## Display
These control the look of the app
- Zoom: Controls how many notes can be shown on screen. At lower values only notes included in the melody will be displayed. At higher values notes significantly higher or lower than the melody will be displayed.

## Lesson Settings menu
The lesson setting screen lets you control the same settings as the main settings screen, as well as settings that only affect the current lesson (e.g. tempo). It can be accessed in 3 ways:

1. After selecting a song on the Song Selection screen
2. By clicking the gear icon in the top bar on the main Lesson Screen (tap anywhere on the screen to show the top bar if not visible)
3. By pressing or swiping back from the main Lesson Screen

### Instrument
Here you can set the playable range of your instrument. This is the highest and lowest note your instrument can produce.

- Range Min: The lowest note your instrument can produce (MIDI value)
- Range Max: The highest note your instrument can product (MIDI value)

### Song
Settings related to the current song. These settings only affect the current lesson.
- Tempo: controls the tempo of the current song in BPM (beats per minute).
